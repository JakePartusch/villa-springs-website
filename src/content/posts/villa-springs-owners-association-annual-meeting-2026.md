**VILLA SPRINGS OWNERS ASSOCIATION**\
**ANNUAL MEETING MINUTES**\
**SPRINGFIELD VOLUNTEER FIRE DEPARTMENT**\
**NOVEMBER 1, 2026**

**CALL TO ORDER:** Meeting called to order by President Bill Gray at 2:00 PM

**OFFICERS PRESENT:** V P Aaron Klusmire, Treasurer Hank Schwarz, Secretary Terry Ficenec

**BOARD PRESENT:** Chad Nolte, Julie Peterson, Tab Newman, Alan Hans, Chuck Phillips

**ATTENDANCE:** 49 homeowners represented (38 present, 11 by proxy) 57% in attendance.

A quorum was present. Members introduced themselves.

**SECRETARY REPORT:** Approval of 2025 Annual meeting Minutes. Secretary Ficenec added (1) correction adding Julie Peterson’s Board acceptance on to the Board.

Minutes were unanimously approved.

**TREASURER REPORT:** Treasurer Schwarz presented a current Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss, and FYE 2027 Budget to all members in attendance. Motion was made by Brenda Promes to increase the “Total Community Events” from $7,525 to $8,000 in aggregate. Motion was “seconded”, voted upon, and passed by Voice.

**ARCHITECTUAL REPORT:** Alan Hans reported that Lot 37’s request for an “Out Building” was approved after modifications were accepted and incorporated into the design.

Alan gave a concise “outline” as to how to submit a request to the Architectural Committee, informing all present that any request should have at least a 30 day “window” for the committee to respond. He also gave an outline as to what the committee typically looks for in a request for approval. Any and all additions or new structures must be approved by the Board. ARTICLES V thru XIV of the Covenants must be complied with.

**FISH AND LAKE REPORT:** Aaron Klusmire reported that it was a “Good Year” for the lake with minimal concerns. Appropriate fish have been added this year, and more will be added later this year to give the lake a “jump start” for next year.

Discussion included Boat Registry. All aquatic vessels must be registered with the Association. Bryan Promes is the contact individual.

Lot Number stickers placed appropriately upon all approved aquatic vessels. Stickers must be placed on the “starboard” side of the vessel. Bryan will provide these upon request.

**SOCIAL COMMITTEE:** Brenda Promes reported that it has been a very good year for community events with significant participation by members. She noted the “Holiday Party” is schedule for December 5th at the American Legion Hall on Pflug Road starting at 6:00PM. Brenda was given a “round of applause” for her efforts to head up this committee.

## OLD BUSINESS

**BOARD MEETINGS:** President Gray noted that the Board met twice in “Closed Sessions” on April 21st and July 19th.

**BRASE v VSOA:** It was noted that the Lawsuit has been dropped. The dock placement has not yet been approved as we are waiting for a qualified survey to be presented to the Board by the Brase family to verify its placement. The Board offers no liability for its current placement. Brase objected stating he believed the “terms of the dropping of the lawsuit” have been met. The Board will investigate its position and reply accordingly.

**BYLAW AND COVENANT REVIEW:** Last year the proposed Bylaw amendments did not meet requirements to be included in the meeting Secretary Ficenec noted there are several antiquated and actual errors in our current Bylaws. The Association currently has a Bylaw review committee headed by Ficenec with Rodney Brase and Chuck Phillips as members.

The committee will be expanded to a total of (5) members with the task to meet in the first quarter of 2027 to determine if amendments or total re-writing is needed. Once the committee has finished its work a Special Meeting will be called to present them to the membership for a vote of approval.

A clear issue of concern is the election of Board Members and Officers.

The Covenants state in Article XIV under “Other Prohibited Uses and Conditions” was reviewed. It was announced to the membership that the Board will attempt to enforce this Article more aggressively in the future.

**SIGNAGE:** Thanks was given to Alan Hans for his work on the new Permanent Entry sign at 132nd and Buffalo Road. It was noted the (3) trees planted were in honor of Don Gray since Gary and Julie Peterson were kind enough to provide the ground for the sign.

Thanks also was given to Bob Shaffer (A new resident at 12707 Sandy Point Road) from Shaffer Painting for taking it upon himself to do a total refurbishing of the “Secondary” sign at 132nd and Lakeview Drive. It looks like brand new…front and back. Thanks also to Glen and Sonya Flint for allowing him to tap into their water source for the power washing.

## NEW BUSINESS

**LOT 66:** President Gray reiterated that the trespassing which has occurred is not the responsibility of the Association as it has occurred on “Private Property”. The Association will assist the Owner by distributing correspondence to our membership that the Owner provides.

**BOAT RAMP ACCESS;** V P Klusmire stated the Board set up a committee to investigate possible means to limit access to only “approved” members. It was decided that “policing” non-approved vessels is the responsibility of all members. It was suggested a sign be placed on the chain post stating the rules and regulations for lake access.

**ROAD MAINTENANCE:** Secretary Ficenec noted the Board has approved “Crack Sealing” for Cottonwood Lane and Lake View Drive for 2026 with ManMann Asphalt in the amount of $8,900.00. ManMann is scheduled to perform this service on November 6th. Members were advised not to come within 100 feet of the service truck doing such work as the sealant will still be soft. With the “cooler” weather the sealant will set up fairly quickly. Please do not drive over freshly laid sealant.

**CORRECTION:** Secretary Ficenec stated ManMann’s warranty for work done on North Shore Drive and Sandy Point was for (1) year. ManMann’s warranty is for (2) years on workmanship and materials….along with a (2) year crack sealing at no addional charge.

Gary Partusch made a Motion to eliminate the “Crack Sealing”. The motion was 2nd by Tab Newman. Discussion followed and a vote was taken. Result: 47 Nay, 2 Aye. The motion failed.

**TRASH:** Treasurer Schwarz stated our cost for trash removal has risen and the new annual fee/lot is $235. Previously it was $220...which fell short of covering 100% of the current cost for trash removal.

**ASSOCIATION “On Line”:** Gary Partusch has agreed to update and maintain this useful and necessary element for the Association. ALL content must be presented to Gary. He will submit them to the VSOA President for review and approval/denial.

The webpage can be found at <https://villaspringslake.com>.

**TREES:** Sarpy County will tour our road system to check for “over hanging” trees that may interfere with their snow removal equipment. Each lot (Owner or Association) will be notified of the immediate need to make provisions to remove limbs noted by the County.

If we do not comply we would be saddled with the expense of providing snow removal from our roads…an expense we have no budget for.

**ELECTION OF OFFICERS:** Secretary Ficenec explained the ambiguity within the Bylaws that leaves the election of Board Members and Offices in confusion. The Board has decided to offer elections “As Per” the Bylaws for the Board members and allow a Membership vote for the Officers

**OFFICERS:** Nothing in the current Bylaws addresses how Officers are to be “Elected”. President Gray and Secretary Ficenec’s 2 year terms have expired. President Gray has chosen not to run. Request for nominations was made to the Membership. Bryan Promes was nominated and accepted. No other nominations were made. Bryan was voted upon unanimously.

Request for Secretary was made. Ficenec accepted. Todd Mach was nominated and accepted. No other nominations were made. A vote was taken. Ficenec received 25 votes and Mach 22. Ficenec will remain as Secretary.

**BOARD MEMBERS:** (3) Board terms expire…Alan Hans, Tab Newman, and Chuck Phillips. All (3) accepted nominations for another (2) year term. Nominations were taken from the floor.

Three additional names were added…Todd Mach, Kerri Plummer-Galecki, and Diana Foral.

A vote was taken. Alan Hans, Chuck Phillips and Todd Mach were voted onto the Board for (2) years.

## OPEN DISCUSSION

One item was brought forward: The identification of non-licensed motorized vehicles on our roads. Russ Matsunami made a motion that was seconded by Dan Lorraine. Much discussion ensued. It was decided the subject matter needed significantly more investigation and was TABLED.

There being no further discussion President Gray adjourned the meeting at 3:51 PM.

Respectfully submitted,

Terry Ficenec

Secretary
